Workout & Clean Diet Guide

Learn how to exercise correctly, eat balanced meals, and build healthy habits every day.

Workout Library

Choose exercises according to your fitness level. Learn the correct technique and allow your body time to recover.

Upper Body

1. Chest Workout

Exercises

Push-ups, bench press, incline dumbbell press, and chest fly.

How to do it

Keep your body stable, lower the weight under control, and push smoothly. For push-ups, keep your body in a straight line and lower your chest toward the floor.

Benefits

Strengthens the chest, shoulders, and triceps and improves pushing strength.

Arms

2. Biceps & Triceps

Exercises

Biceps curls, hammer curls, triceps pushdowns, and overhead extensions.

How to do it

Keep your elbows controlled near your body during curls. Extend your arms smoothly during triceps exercises without locking your joints forcefully.

Benefits

Develops arm strength and helps with lifting, carrying, and other daily activities.

Lower Body

3. Leg Workout

Exercises

Squats, lunges, leg press, hamstring curls, and calf raises.

How to do it

Keep your feet stable, bend your knees comfortably, and maintain balance. Start with bodyweight movements before adding weights.

Benefits

Strengthens the legs and glutes and supports balance, mobility, and everyday movement.

Upper Body

4. Shoulder Workout

Exercises

Shoulder press, lateral raises, rear-delt fly, and face pulls.

How to do it

Use light weights at first. Lift with control, avoid swinging, and do not force painful overhead movements.

Benefits

Builds shoulder strength and helps improve controlled arm movements.

Cardiovascular Fitness

5. Cardio Workout

Exercises

Brisk walking, cycling, treadmill jogging, and elliptical training.

How to do it

Begin at a comfortable pace and increase duration or intensity gradually. Choose an activity that you enjoy and can maintain.

Benefits

Improves stamina, supports heart health, and may help with stress management.

Flexibility

6. Yoga & Stretching

Exercises

Cat-cow, child's pose, gentle hamstring stretches, and downward-facing dog.

How to do it

Breathe slowly and move gently into each pose. Never force a stretch or continue through pain.

Benefits

Can improve flexibility, balance, relaxation, and body awareness.

Core Strength

7. Abs & Core Workout

Exercises

Planks, dead bugs, bird dogs, and controlled crunches.

How to do it

Keep your trunk stable, breathe normally, and focus on good form rather than rushing through repetitions.

Benefits

Supports posture, balance, and stability. Ab exercises alone do not specifically remove belly fat.

Recovery

8. Rest & Recovery

Daily habits

Get enough sleep, drink water, eat balanced meals, and schedule rest days.

How it helps

Muscles adapt to exercise when training is combined with adequate nutrition and recovery.

Benefits

Supports performance, energy, and consistency in your fitness routine.

Clean Diet Guide

A balanced diet provides energy, protein, healthy fats, vitamins, minerals, and fibre. You do not need to avoid rice or chapati to eat healthily.

Protein-Rich Foods

Examples: Eggs, milk, curd, paneer, tofu, dal, beans, chickpeas, and soy.

Purpose: Protein helps repair and build muscle when combined with appropriate resistance training.

Carbohydrates for Energy

Examples: Rice, oats, chapati, millets, potatoes, and fruit.

Purpose: Carbohydrates provide energy for workouts, studying, work, and daily activities.

Healthy Fats

Examples: Nuts, seeds, peanuts, and suitable cooking oils.

Purpose: Healthy fats support normal body functions and help absorb certain vitamins.

Fruit & Vegetables

Examples: Bananas, oranges, apples, leafy greens, carrots, and seasonal vegetables.

Purpose: Provide fibre, vitamins, minerals, and variety in your meals.

Diet Plans for Your Fitness Goal

Muscle Gain

Eat enough food to support training and recovery. Include protein in regular meals and gradually increase strength-training difficulty.

Meal ideas: Oats with curd and banana, eggs or paneer, rice with dal, and vegetables with dinner.

Weight Loss

Aim for a moderate calorie deficit while eating enough protein and nutritious foods. Avoid crash diets and skipping meals to compensate for eating.

Meal ideas: Vegetable oats, dal with rice and vegetables, fruit, curd, and a protein-rich dinner.

Healthy Eating

Choose a variety of foods and balance your meals with vegetables, protein, carbohydrates, and healthy fats.

Meal ideas: Idli with sambar, rice with dal and vegetables, fruit, nuts, and curd or another protein source.

Daily Fitness Tips

  • Warm up for 5–10 minutes before challenging exercise.
  • Learn proper technique before increasing weights.
  • Drink water regularly throughout the day.
  • Most adults should aim for 7–9 hours of sleep.
  • Take rest when needed and avoid training through sharp pain.
  • Exercise can support mood and stress management, alongside other healthy habits.
